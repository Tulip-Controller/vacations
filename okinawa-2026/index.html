Good to know for November
  • A child seat is required by law for kids under 6. Ask the rental car company for one.
  • Most beaches end lifeguard and jellyfish-net season in late October. Water is still about 25 °C, so wading and rock-pooling are fine. Swim only where it's calm.
  • Shuri Castle's main hall has been under reconstruction since the 2019 fire. Check what's open before you go.
  • Hours change often. Check the Maps listing on the day.
